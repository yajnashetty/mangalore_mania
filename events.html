Events Across Mangalore 🎉

From traditional rituals to modern festivals, experience the vibrant pulse of Mangalore.

Mangalore Dasara

Grand procession with Navaratri decorations and cultural performances at Kudroli Temple.

Karavali Utsav

A coastal cultural fest with traditional music, dance, stalls and local crafts.

Yakshagana Evenings

Traditional theater showcasing mythological stories with vibrant costumes and drums.

Temple Car Festivals

Annual rathotsava at Kadri, Mangaladevi, and other major temples.

Kambala (Buffalo Race)

Traditional rural sport of buffalo racing held in water-filled paddy fields.

Beach Festivals

Celebrations on Panambur Beach with sand art, kite shows, and music.
